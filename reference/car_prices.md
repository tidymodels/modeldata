# Kelly Blue Book resale data for 2005 model year GM cars

Kuiper (2008) collected data on Kelly Blue Book resale data for 804 GM
cars (2005 model year).

## Source

Kuiper, S. (2008). Introduction to Multiple Regression: How Much Is Your
Car Worth?, *Journal of Statistics Education*, Vol. 16
<https://jse.amstat.org/jse_archive.htm#2008>.

## Value

- car_prices:

  data frame of the suggested retail price (column `Price`) and various
  characteristics of each car (columns `Mileage`, `Cylinder`, `Doors`,
  `Cruise`, `Sound`, `Leather`, `Buick`, `Cadillac`, `Chevy`, `Pontiac`,
  `Saab`, `Saturn`, `convertible`, `coupe`, `hatchback`, `sedan` and
  `wagon`)
